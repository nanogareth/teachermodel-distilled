DPIA screening

Screen the programme's processing.

Two or more criteria usually indicate a DPIA is required (WP248 rev.01).

Each answer is pre-filled from the processing facts below. Confirm or change every one, then record your decision.

Processing facts

Summary

PurposeInstructor clearance and coaching
Data subjectsInstructors (employees and partner staff)

Categories

  • identity reference
  • observation scores
  • clearance level

Screening criteria and decision

Instructors are scored against a rubric and given clearance levels.

Rules compute eligibility; a named person signs every clearance and level change.

Observations of delivery are recorded on a schedule (observation design).

No special-category or criminal-offence data; records hold references, roles, scores and dates.

A small cohort of instructors in one organisation.

Observations, roster and clearance events come from this programme only; nothing is joined to HR or external data.

Employees are listed as vulnerable data subjects because of the employer power imbalance.

Rules are deterministic; no model scores or profiles people.

Clearance decides teaching assignments, not access to a service, benefit or contract.

Two or more criteria usually indicate a DPIA is required.

Why this decision, in a sentence or two.

Your sign-off is recorded as an approval only when the DPO decision is "dpia-not-required". Any other decision is recorded as a request for changes and does not complete this step.
