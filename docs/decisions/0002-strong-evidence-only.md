# 0002 Change rules only on strong evidence

## Context
The research rated findings strong, moderate or weak. Several popular practices (live coding,
certified-trainer badges, microteaching effect sizes) rest on weak evidence.

## Decision
Only findings rated strong change `standards/`: multi-rater observation and audit (MET), continuous
coaching (Kraft et al. 2018), expertise reversal, peer instruction. Moderate and weak findings are
listed as deferred in the spec and in `docs/evidence/`, each with the change it would make.

## Consequences
A deferred finding is promoted by a new decision citing new evidence, which can include this
system's own pilot data. Context changes (0001) and operational defaults (audit rate, cadence)
are labelled as such and do not claim evidence.
