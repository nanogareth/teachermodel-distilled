# 0005 Separation from employment performance management

## Context
Clearance and coaching records are performance data about named employees. Coaching works best
when instructors expose weaknesses; feeding the same records into performance reviews changes
what instructors are willing to show, and the privacy notice must state the use.

## Options
1. Training-function use only; performance reviews receive clearance status, not scores.
2. Full records available to performance management.

## Decision
Your observation scores, evidence notes, self-assessments and coaching records are used only by the technical training function, to coach you and to decide clearances. Your manager and anyone running a performance review see only your clearance status: which modules you are cleared to teach, and until when. They do not see scores, notes or coaching records.

## Consequences
Option 1, decided by the head of training on 2026-10-24. The Decision section above is
written to the instructor, because the privacy notice quotes it verbatim (`setup.notice_text`).
The role table already matches it: managers and partner leads see clearance status, not scores.
A future change to this use needs a new decision and a new notice version sent before it applies.
