**To:** `<it_email>`
**Subject:** Request: Mail.Send for the training programme mailbox only

Hello,

The training function's app registration (client id `<TEACHERMODEL_GRAPH_CLIENT_ID>`) already reads the programme
mailbox `<mailbox>` with `Mail.Read`, limited to that mailbox. We would like it to **send** from that one mailbox too.

**What we ask for**

- The application permission `Mail.Send`, admin-consented, on the same app registration.
- Scoped to the programme mailbox `<mailbox>` only. Either:
  - **RBAC for Applications** (preferred): assign the role *Application Mail.Send* to the app's service principal with a
    management scope containing only `<mailbox>`; or
  - an **application access policy**: `New-ApplicationAccessPolicy -AppId <client id> -PolicyScopeGroupId <a
    mail-enabled security group holding only the mailbox> -AccessRight RestrictAccess`.
- Please confirm the scope with `Test-ServicePrincipalAuthorization` (RBAC) or `Test-ApplicationAccessPolicy` against
  `<mailbox>` (allowed) and one other mailbox (denied), and send us the output.

**Why**

Each person in the training function gets one page about what they must act on: the head of training a weekly page,
the coordinator a board when something is open, an observer their scoring page, an instructor their own record. Today
the head forwards these by hand. Pages are generated from the record and filtered per role before they leave: they
carry ids rather than names, and the coordinator never receives scores.

**What it sends, and how much**

- From `<mailbox>` only, to people in the organisation; no external recipients.
- Low volume: in a pilot cohort, well under 100 messages a week, sent by a scheduled job, never in bulk.
- Each send is recorded in the training function's private store (recipient, page, time), so it can be audited.

**What it does not need**

No `Mail.ReadWrite`, no access to any other mailbox, no delegated permissions, no change to the existing `Mail.Read`
scope.

**Removing it**

Remove the role assignment or the policy, or the permission on the app. The job then stops sending and pages go back
to being forwarded by hand; nothing else breaks.

Thank you,
`<the head of training>`
