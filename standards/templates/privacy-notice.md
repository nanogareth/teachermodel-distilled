## What we hold

<!-- element: categories-of-data -->
$held

We hold the minimum a decision needs. Observation notes and recording links stay in the private
store; the event log holds references to them, not the content.

<!-- element: source -->
Some of this comes from you. The rest comes from others: observers' scores and notes, roster data
from your organisation, and delivery outcomes, which we hold only as totals and averages. None of
it comes from publicly accessible sources (legal review).

<!-- element: statutory-or-contractual-requirement -->
We need the roster details and the evidence above to clear you to teach and to schedule you. If
they are missing we cannot clear you or schedule you to teach. Whether that is a statutory or a
contractual requirement depends on how you are engaged (legal review).

## Recordings

Learners appear in session recordings. Recordings stay where they are; this system stores a link
and timestamps, not a copy. Observers use recordings only for observation and coaching.

## Your rights

<!-- element: rights -->
- You can see your full record.
- You can reply to every decision about you.
- You can appeal a decision to the head of training.
- You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or, where it applies, portability.
  Requests are handled under the law that applies where you work (legal review).

<!-- element: right-to-object -->
You can object at any time to processing that rests on our legitimate interests. We then stop
unless we show compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or we need the data for
legal claims.
