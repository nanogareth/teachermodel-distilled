# Coaching protocol (W7)

Status: draft, framework 0.2.0. Basis: coaching meta-analysis of 60 causal studies, +0.49 SD on
practice, +0.18 SD on achievement; virtual coaching comparable; smaller effects in larger
programmes (Kraft, Blazar & Hogan 2018). K-12 population: the mechanism is adopted, the effect
size is not promised.

Every active instructor is in a cycle. One cycle:

1. **Observe.** Full or 15-minute, live or from a recording. Scored on the rubric.
2. **Feedback.** Within 5 working days. One or two focus domains, each with an evidence note.
3. **Practise.** One concrete practice task per focus domain, done before the next delivery.
4. **Re-observe.** The focus domains are scored again; the cycle closes with the change recorded.

Cadence: standard every 91 days; triggered every 30 days (rules.yaml `coaching`).
A coach's observations count as at most one of the two observers for a clearance.
